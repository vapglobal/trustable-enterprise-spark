# Trustable Builder Growth Organism

## Goal

Show how Trustable begins with one useful, measurable solution and grows into a governed enterprise app-building network. Connect individual learning, organizational adoption, OASA graph growth, management proof, sales expansion, and customer economics in one interactive experience.

## What will be built

1. **Dedicated Growth workspace**
   - Add a `Growth` destination inside the signed-in workspace and a concise public landing-page section that introduces the progression model.
   - Keep the full interactive experience inside the protected workspace.

2. **Synchronized Level 1 / 5 / 10 control**
   - A prominent segmented control changes every panel together.
   - Level 1: approachable guided builder, one advocate, essential tools, strong coaching.
   - Level 5: team graphlets, parallel work, richer calibration, management reporting, expanded advocates.
   - Level 10: architect workspace with multi-harnesses, parallel loop sets, topology controls, advanced governance inspection, and organization-wide views.
   - Higher levels can inspect lower-level Trustables while all authority remains subject to tenant roles, delegated approvals, and customer contract gates.

3. **One connected visual model**
   - Build an animated organization graph showing users, Trustables, KPI value, and the synaptic bridges created when one solution saves another person time.
   - Use Bob, Diane, Hubert, Puter, and memorable callsign-style sample identities such as Blue Actual, Major Merge, and Captain Callback.
   - Pair the graph with a compact multi-line progression chart for builders, minutes returned, graph links, advocates, and projected contract efficiency.

4. **Certification and incentive system**
   - Show auditable minutes saved, solutions built, people helped, validation checks, and learning checkpoints.
   - Present next unlock, progress, certification portability, team recognition, and management visibility without bypassing enterprise controls.
   - Include self-reinforcing prompts that reward safe exploration and measurable value rather than raw activity.

5. **Economics and sales bridge**
   - Add sliders for active builders, Level 5 graduates, average minutes saved, solution reuse, and loaded hourly cost.
   - Calculate time returned, estimated value, network reach, and an example volume discount at each 25 Level 5 builders.
   - Clearly label discount rules, capacity effects, network priority, dedicated bandwidth, and additional advocates as an illustrative commercial model until approved contracts exist.

6. **Truthful data boundaries**
   - Use existing tenant runs for live minutes/value when available.
   - Label certification policy, level entitlements, OASA negotiation, model-cost pass-through, discounts, and autonomous advocate expansion as `Reference architecture` or `Illustrative program model`.
   - Do not alter protected EngineWare or security files and do not invent callable endpoints.

7. **Verification and release**
   - Add route-specific metadata and confidential ownership markings.
   - Verify desktop/mobile, light/dark, keyboard operation, no overflow, synchronized level changes, calculator math, and signed-in navigation.
   - Update the completion ledger and roadmap with accurate implementation and verification evidence, then publish the verified update.

## Technical approach

- Implement a focused reusable frontend module in `src/components/trustable/` and a new authenticated route in `src/routes/_authenticated/`.
- Reuse existing workspace data, design tokens, buttons, tooltips, proof labels, report actions, and role-gated navigation.
- Keep all commercial assumptions in a visible configuration object so the prototype math is transparent and inspectable.
- Use SVG/React for the graph and Recharts for the progression chart; no backend schema or protected contract changes.

## Acceptance checks

- One action switches Level 1, 5, and 10 across the entire interface.
- Each level has visibly different tools, controls, advocates, graph complexity, filters, and explanations.
- Changing a calculator input updates all derived values and the graph/chart immediately.
- Live tenant metrics and illustrative projections cannot be confused.
- All controls work by keyboard and remain readable at mobile and desktop sizes.
- Every screen retains the Trustable confidentiality notice and Live/Reference truth labels.
